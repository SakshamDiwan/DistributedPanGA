\subsection{K-mer extraction}\label{sec:alg:extraction}

Extraction reproduces FastGA's k-mer sampling exactly. Each rank scans a
contiguous range of whole contigs, balanced by length, in windows of up to
10\,Mbp. The start $j$ of a 12-mer is selected when the 12-mer is a closed
syncmer: the minimum of its five canonical 8-mers, under FastGA's fixed
(non-lexicographic) order, occurs at its first or last 8-mer position.
With ties, an occurrence at either end suffices.

Each selected position yields up to two records: the forward 40-mer
starting at $j$ and the reverse complement of the 40-mer ending at the last
base of the syncmer; a strand is skipped where its 40-mer would leave the
contig. A record stores the 2-bit packed k-mer with its contig, position
and strand.

Every window after the first is extended 40 bases to the left, so that
reverse-complement k-mers and forward k-mers starting before the window
lie inside it. Only a forward k-mer that runs past the right edge of a
non-final window is read separately from the GDB. The scan state carries
across windows, so the selected positions and the order of emitted records
do not depend on the window size.
